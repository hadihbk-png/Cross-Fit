# Photos

Drop your own photos here and the homepage uses them automatically (refresh the page, no restart needed).
Until a photo exists, the site shows its built-in graphics instead.

| File | Used for | Suggested size |
|---|---|---|
| `hero.jpg` | Full-width homepage background (darkened automatically so text stays readable) | 2400 × 1400, landscape |
| `classes/wod.jpg` | CrossFit WOD card | 1200 × 600 |
| `classes/olympic.jpg` | Olympic Lifting card | 1200 × 600 |
| `classes/foundations.jpg` | Foundations card | 1200 × 600 |
| `classes/open-gym.jpg` | Open Gym card | 1200 × 600 |
| `coaches/khalid-nasser.jpg` | Coach photo (cropped to a circle) | 600 × 600, face centred |
| `coaches/maya-ross.jpg` | Coach photo | 600 × 600 |
| `coaches/tom-becker.jpg` | Coach photo | 600 × 600 |
| `coaches/noor-saleh.jpg` | Coach photo | 600 × 600 |

`.webp`, `.jpg`, `.jpeg` and `.png` all work. Keep each file under ~400 KB so pages load fast on phones,
and only use photos you own or have permission to use (with permission from anyone pictured).

The photos shipped with the site are openly licensed from Wikimedia Commons and credited on `/credits`
(from `credits.json`). When you replace one with your own photo, remove its entry from `credits.json`.
